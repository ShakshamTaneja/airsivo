## Airsivo 1.0

Your tablet. Your Mac's second screen.

**What's new**
- Wireless extended or mirrored display for your Mac on any Android tablet
- Touch control: tap, drag, scroll and right-click
- Sound follows the screen: apps on the tablet play through the tablet
- Up to 4K at 60 fps with hardware HEVC, about 45 ms average latency
- No account, no cloud, no tracking

**Downloads**
- `Airsivo-1.0.0.pkg` for macOS 14.4 or later (Apple silicon and Intel)
- `Airsivo-1.0.0.apk` for Android 8.0 or later
