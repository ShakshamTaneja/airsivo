AIRSIVO
SECURITY AND VULNERABILITY DISCLOSURE POLICY

Last updated: 17 October 2026

1. OUR APPROACH. Airsivo is designed to process the minimum data necessary, to retain nothing, to request only the operating-system permissions it needs, and to communicate only between your own Devices on your local network.

2. KNOWN LIMITATIONS. You should be aware that:
(a) streams are currently sent over your local network without encryption;
(b) any tablet on your network running Airsivo can connect to a Mac running Airsivo while the Mac app is open; and
(c) a connected tablet can view and control the Mac.
Use Airsivo only on trusted networks, quit Airsivo on your Mac when you are not using it, and keep your operating systems up to date.

3. REPORTING A VULNERABILITY. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in Airsivo, please report it privately to legal@imperialecc.com with a description, steps to reproduce, affected versions and your contact details. Please do not disclose it publicly until we have had a reasonable opportunity to fix it.

4. OUR COMMITMENT. We will aim to acknowledge your report within five (5) business days, keep you informed of progress, and credit you (with your permission) when a fix is released.

5. SAFE HARBOUR. We will not pursue legal action against researchers who, in good faith: test only against Devices they own or are authorised to test; avoid privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption; do not exploit a vulnerability beyond what is necessary to demonstrate it; and comply with this Policy and applicable law. This safe harbour does not authorise any activity that breaches the law or the rights of third parties.

6. NO BOUNTY. Imperial ECC does not currently operate a paid bug-bounty programme.
